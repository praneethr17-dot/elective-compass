Term 5 electives start 19 Oct. The bids you place now shape what's still possible in later terms.

Elective Compass is an independent student tool. Its core feature, PlanCheck, reviews your elective plan before you bid. It doesn't predict bid outcomes or seats.

Try the PlanCheck demo

The problem

Plan gaps tend to surface after the bids are in.

01

Uneven track coverage is spotted late

For example, Term 5 has no designated core course for Public Policy or GCC Leadership.

02

Sequencing rules are easy to miss

  • A course split across Terms 5 and 7
  • A course mandatory for a concentration
  • Two versions of the same modelling course
03

What drives choices lives outside the outlines

Bid history, workload and faculty style aren't in the course outlines.

How PlanCheck works

Enter your plan. Get three checks back.

You enter

  • Career track (plus an optional one-line goal)
  • Completed electives
  • Points spent
  • Proposed Term 5 electives with bids

PlanCheck scans all 105 electives and returns:

Rules
  • Can your remaining terms still reach 18 electives and 33–36 credits?
  • Do remaining points cover at least 100 per elective needed?
  • Any sequencing issues?
Fit

Each course's relevance to your track, quoting its outline. Labelled “based on course outline only”.

Missed options

Relevant electives you didn't list, with coverage warnings.

Every flag cites the outline behind it.
